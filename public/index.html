HOW TO USE IT

Three moves. No guessing.

Each workspace section answers a specific engineering question and points you toward the next useful action.

01
UnderstandStart at Overview, then open History and Structure to learn the repository before reading derived signals.
02
ValidateUse Quality and Runtime to check repeated changes, tests, telemetry, deployments and revision provenance.
03
Output & inspectGenerate Report or Insights, then use Evidence Explorer whenever you need to verify a claim.
WORKSPACE MAP

What each section is for

01Overview

Your current snapshot, run boundary and a compact read of the repository.

02–03History & Structure

What changed over time and how the codebase shape moved with it.

04–05Quality & Runtime

Where repeated changes, tests, telemetry and deployments provide supporting evidence.

06–08Report, Interview & Insights

Turn the current evidence snapshot into usable outputs without hiding uncertainty.

09–10Evidence & Contributors

Trace records back to their source and understand author-level change history.

11Operations

Check the running service separately from repository evidence.

Evidence remains separate from interpretation. Autopsy does not present a heuristic as intent, causality or impact without supporting evidence.
